# The Desire of Ages

**Author:** Ellen G. White

A devotional account of the life and ministry of Jesus Christ.

## Reading guide

Notice how Jesus treats people, responds to suffering, teaches about God's kingdom, and remains faithful through the final events of his earthly ministry.

## Reflection

- What do Jesus' actions reveal about God's character?
- Which encounter or teaching speaks to you most?
- How might Christ's example shape your relationships?

This page is a reading guide, not the complete book text.
