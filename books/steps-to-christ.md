# Steps to Christ

**Author:** Ellen G. White

A short, devotional guide to a personal relationship with Jesus.

## Reading guide

The chapters explore God's love, repentance, faith, consecration, prayer, and the joy of Christian growth. Reflect on how each lesson relates to the life and character of Jesus.

## Reflection

- What does this chapter teach about Christ?
- What invitation or promise stands out?
- How could you put this lesson into practice?

This page is a reading guide, not the complete book text.
