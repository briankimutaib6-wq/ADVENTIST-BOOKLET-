# The Great Controversy

**Author:** Ellen G. White

A historical and theological work describing the conflict between good and evil and its implications for faith and hope.

## Reading guide

As you read, consider choices made by people and communities, and keep themes of Christ's character, Scripture, freedom, and hope in view.

## Reflection

- Which events or ideas are new to you?
- How does the book describe the character of Christ?
- What does the reading suggest about hope and faithfulness?

This page is a reading guide, not the complete book text.
